# Usability test forms

For the [usability test](usability-test.md). Print one set per participant: the session sheet
and the observation sheet are for the moderator, and the SUS questionnaire is for the
participant. Cut the task cards apart to hand over, one at a time.

Participant: **P__** (a number, never a name) · Date: ________ · Phone: ________

---

## Session sheet

**Agreed to take part, with notes taken but no recording:** yes / no

**Background**
- How many plants, indoors and outdoors: ______________________
- How they remember to look after them now: ______________________
- Phone and browser: ______________________
- Used a plant care app before? Which? ______________________

---

## Task cards

1. You've just installed this app for your household. Get it ready to use, with yourself as
   the person in charge.
2. Add your home, and the room where you keep most of your plants. That room has a big window
   facing south.
3. Add one of your own plants that lives in that room. Use its real kind, if you know it.
4. Some plants need watering today. You've just watered all the ones in the living room. Let
   the app know.
5. You watered the aloe yesterday but forgot to note it. Add it now, and that the soil was
   still damp when you checked.
6. You're away until Monday. Make sure the app doesn't nag you about the dragon tree before
   then.
7. Find out whether the golden pothos gets enough light where it is, and what you could do
   about it.
8. Take a photo of something near you as if it were the avocado seedling, add it to that
   plant, and make it show only the middle of the photo.
9. You've moved the flaming Katy into a bigger pot, 17 cm across. Update it.
10. Is the golden pothos safe to keep if you had a cat?
11. Someone you live with wants to help with the watering. Let them in.
12. You'd like a reminder on your phone each morning about what needs doing. Set that up.

## SUS questionnaire

*For the participant, straight after the tasks.* For each statement, circle how much you
agree, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). Answer quickly: your first
reaction is the right one. Please answer every one; if one doesn't apply, circle 3.

| | Statement | Strongly disagree | | | | Strongly agree |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | I think that I would like to use this app frequently. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 2 | I found the app unnecessarily complex. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 3 | I thought the app was easy to use. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 4 | I think that I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use this app. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 5 | I found the various functions in this app were well integrated. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 6 | I thought there was too much inconsistency in this app. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 7 | I would imagine that most people would learn to use this app very quickly. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 8 | I found the app very cumbersome to use. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 9 | I felt very confident using the app. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| 10 | I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this app. | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

*The System Usability Scale, by John Brooke (1986), with "system" read as "app", as is
common. Free to use. Scoring is in the [test plan](usability-test.md#scoring-and-rating).*

---

## Debrief

Ask after the questionnaire. Note their answers in their own words.

1. What was the easiest thing you did? And the hardest?
2. Was anything different from what you expected?
3. Was there a word or a screen you didn't understand?
4. Would you use this for your own plants? What would stop you?
5. If you could change one thing, what would it be?
6. Anything else?
